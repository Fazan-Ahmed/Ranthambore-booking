About Ranthambore Ticket Booking


Ranthambore Ticket Booking is an independently operated private safari and travel booking assistance service based in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan, India.

We assist travellers in planning their visit to Ranthambore by providing information and assistance related to safari booking requests, accommodation enquiries, transportation and other travel arrangements.

Important: Ranthambore Ticket Booking is not a government organization, is not the Rajasthan Forest Department, and is not the official website or authority of Ranthambore National Park. We are not affiliated with or representatives of any government authority.

What We Do

We assist travellers with various Ranthambore travel requirements, including:

  • Safari booking assistance
  • Safari permit and booking-related assistance
  • Hotel and resort enquiries
  • Transportation assistance
  • Group tour arrangements
  • Customized travel planning
  • General information about safari options and booking procedures

Our role is to assist customers with their booking and travel arrangements. Safari permits, vehicle availability, safari zones, entry and other park-related services remain subject to the applicable booking system, availability and rules of the relevant authorities.

Our Approach

We aim to provide customers with:

  • Clear information about our services
  • Transparent pricing and applicable charges
  • Accurate booking conditions
  • Responsive customer support
  • Clear information about what is and is not included in a booking

Submitting a booking request does not automatically guarantee confirmation. A safari booking is considered confirmed only after the applicable booking and payment requirements have been completed and confirmation has been received.

Safari Booking Information

Safari availability may vary depending on the selected date, safari type, vehicle availability, zone availability and applicable booking conditions.

Wildlife sightings are naturally unpredictable. We do not guarantee tiger sightings or sightings of any particular animal during a safari.

Similarly, requesting a particular safari zone, vehicle or safari timing does not necessarily guarantee its availability. Availability remains subject to the applicable booking system and relevant authorities.

Where a booking requires information, permits or services from a relevant authority or third-party service provider, we may assist customers with the applicable booking process.

We do not represent ourselves as an official government booking website or as an authorized representative of the Rajasthan Forest Department unless such authorization is specifically documented.

Our Commitment to Customers

Our goal is to make planning a Ranthambore visit easier by providing straightforward information and practical booking assistance.

We encourage customers to review the pricing, booking conditions, cancellation and refund policy, inclusions and service details before making a payment.

If you have any questions regarding a booking or our services, please contact us directly before making a payment.
