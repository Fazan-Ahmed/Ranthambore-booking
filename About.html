We Are a Private Travel Company

Ranthambore Ticket Booking is an independent, privately owned tour & travel business. We are not a government body, not the Rajasthan Forest Department, and not the official Ranthambore National Park authority. We work as a booking and travel-assistance service — we purchase genuine safari permits from the Forest Department on your behalf, and also arrange hotels, transport, and complete tour packages. Official park permits and gate entry are always controlled by the Forest Department; our role is to make the booking process simple, fast, and hassle-free for you.

Our Story

Your Ranthambore Safari Partner

Ranthambore Ticket Booking is focused on helping travellers plan their visit to Ranthambore National Park with simple and convenient booking support.

We assist travellers with safari ticket and permit booking, hotel and resort reservations, group tour packages, and customised travel options. Our goal is to make your Ranthambore trip easier to plan, so you can focus on enjoying the wildlife and natural beauty of the park.

Whether you're planning a family trip, a group tour, or a wildlife getaway, we're here to help you organise your Ranthambore experience with clear information and friendly support.
