A private tour & travel company built by locals who know Ranthambore — helping travellers book safari tickets, hotels, and complete trip packages without the confusion.

Home About Us

We Are a Private Travel Company

Ranthambore Ticket Booking is an independent, privately owned tour & travel business. We are not a government body, not the Rajasthan Forest Department, and not the official Ranthambore National Park authority. We work as a booking and travel-assistance service — we purchase genuine safari permits from the Forest Department on your behalf, and also arrange hotels, transport, and complete tour packages. Official park permits and gate entry are always controlled by the Forest Department; our role is to make the booking process simple, fast, and hassle-free for you.

Our Story

Local People, Real Experience

We started in Sawai Madhopur in 2012 with one simple idea — travellers coming to see the tigers of Ranthambore shouldn't have to struggle with confusing permit systems, unreliable hotels, or overpriced last-minute bookings. Over the years we've grown from a small ticketing desk near the park gate into a full-service travel company.

Today we handle everything a visitor needs for a Ranthambore trip — safari ticket & permit booking (Gypsy and Canter), hotel and resort reservations, group tour packages, and customised family trips. Every naturalist and driver we work with is local, experienced, and genuinely passionate about the park and its wildlife.
