Refund/Cancellation Policy


Last Updated: 7 October 2026

Ranthambore Ticket Booking is an independent private travel and safari booking assistance service for travellers visiting Ranthambore, Sawai Madhopur. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department or any government authority.

Safari permits and bookings are subject to availability and the applicable rules of the relevant booking system and authority. This policy explains the cancellation and refund process applicable to safari bookings handled through our service.

Quick Summary

  • An enquiry (form, call or WhatsApp) is not a confirmed booking.
  • 30 days or more before the safari: 75% refund. More than 72 hours but less than 30 days: 50% refund. 72 hours or less: no refund.
  • If we cannot confirm your booking after you have paid, you get a full refund of the amount paid.
  • No tiger or other wildlife sighting is guaranteed, and a missed sighting is not a reason for refund.

1. Safari Cancellation & Refund

For a confirmed safari booking that you cancel, the cancellation and refund schedule is:

  • Cancellation 30 days or more before the visit date: 75% refund of the eligible amount.
  • Cancellation more than 72 hours but less than 30 days before the visit date: 50% refund of the eligible amount.
  • Cancellation 72 hours or less before the visit date, or no-show: No refund.

The “eligible amount” is the amount paid for the safari booking, excluding any charge that cannot be refunded. Certain permit, government, vehicle, guide, processing or other applicable charges may be non-refundable. Any such charge is communicated to you before payment.

If the relevant authority or booking system applies different rules to a particular booking, those rules will be communicated to you before payment.

2. Booking Confirmation

Submitting an enquiry through our website, WhatsApp, email or telephone does not by itself constitute a confirmed safari booking.

A safari booking is considered confirmed only after the required availability, booking information and applicable payment or confirmation requirements have been completed and confirmation has been communicated to you.

3. Cancellation Request

Customers who wish to cancel a confirmed safari booking should contact us as soon as possible and provide their booking details.

The date and time when the cancellation request is received is considered when determining the applicable cancellation category.

Refunds will be processed according to the applicable cancellation and booking rules.

4. Weather & Natural Conditions

Safari operations may be affected by weather, forest conditions, safety restrictions, government directions or other circumstances beyond our control.

If a safari is cancelled or affected because of such circumstances, any refund will be handled according to the applicable rules of the relevant authority or booking system.

5. Wildlife Sightings

Ranthambore Ticket Booking does not guarantee tiger sightings or sightings of any particular animal.

Wildlife movement is natural and unpredictable. A safari booking provides access to the safari experience subject to the applicable rules and availability, but does not guarantee a particular wildlife sighting. Not seeing a particular animal is not a reason for refund.

6. Refund Processing

Where a refund is applicable, the eligible refund amount will be processed through the applicable payment method or booking process.

The time required for the refund to reach the customer's account may vary depending on the payment provider, bank, and relevant booking authority.

7. Third-Party Services

Where a booking involves a hotel, transport provider, guide, safari operator or other third-party service provider, the cancellation and refund conditions applicable to that service may also apply.

Relevant terms will be communicated to the customer wherever applicable.

8. Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information required for their booking, including:

  • Full name
  • Contact number
  • Safari date
  • Number of visitors
  • Required identification details
  • Other information necessary for the booking

Incorrect or incomplete information may affect the booking or any applicable cancellation/refund process.

9. Availability & Permit Allocation

Safari availability, permit issuance, zone allocation, vehicle allocation, guide allocation and other operational matters are subject to the applicable booking system and relevant authorities.

Availability is not guaranteed until the booking has been confirmed.

10. Booking Not Confirmed

If payment has been received from the customer but we are unable to confirm or complete the safari booking from our side due to permit unavailability, booking-system availability, or other reasons preventing us from completing the booking, the full amount received from the customer for that unconfirmed booking will be refunded, with no deduction.

The refund will be processed through the applicable payment method or booking process. The time required for the refund to reach the customer's account may vary depending on the payment method and bank.

11. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

For a cancellation or refund request, please provide:

  • Customer name
  • Booking/enquiry reference, if available
  • Safari date
  • Contact number
  • Reason for cancellation
  • Payment or booking details, where required

Our team will review the request and communicate the applicable refund process.

12. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services, booking procedures or applicable rules.

The cancellation and refund conditions applicable to a booking will remain subject to the rules of the relevant booking and permit system.
