Rules & Guidelines

Important Safari Instructions

Follow these guidelines for a safe and responsible tiger safari experience.

1
Carry Valid Photo ID Carry the valid government-issued identification document required for your safari booking and park entry. Applicable age and entry requirements may vary according to current park rules.
2
Dress in Muted ColorsWear earthy, muted tones — olive, khaki, brown. Avoid white and bright colors that disturb wildlife and reduce sighting chances.
3
No Noise or Flash PhotographyPlease follow applicable park rules regarding noise and photography.
4
Stay in the VehicleNever step out of the safari vehicle inside the park under any circumstances. This is non-negotiable for your safety.
5
No Plastic or LitteringPlease follow applicable park rules regarding plastic, food items and littering.
6
Report 30 Mins EarlyArrive at the safari gate at least 30 minutes before departure.
7
No Feeding WildlifeDo not feed or approach wildlife. Follow applicable park safety rules.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ranthambore Safari Booking.

Fill our booking form above or click the WhatsApp button. Share your name, date, zone preference, and number of guests. Our team will contact you within 2 hours.

A gypsy is an open safari vehicle that can accommodate up to 06 visitors, while a canter is a larger open safari vehicle that can accommodate up to 20 visitors.
A gypsy offers a smaller-group safari experience, while a canter is suitable for visitors who prefer a larger group safari option.

Zones 3 and 4 are popular choices among visitors because they include areas around PADAM TALAO, RAJBAGH TALAO and MALIK TALAO. Zones 1, 2 and 5 may also offer wildlife sightings depending on current conditions.

October to June is the best season. Summer (April–June) has the highest sighting rate as tigers come to water bodies. October–February offers pleasant weather. The park closes from July 1 to September 30 (monsoon season).

Advance booking is recommended, especially during busy travel periods, because safari permits and vehicle availability are limited.

Location

Find Ranthambore National Park

Located in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan — Approximately 165 km from Jaipur, Approximately 350 km from Delhi.

By Train

Sawai Madhopur Railway Station is the nearest. Direct trains from Delhi, Jaipur, and Mumbai.

By Road

165 km from Jaipur (Approximately~3.5 hrs), 352 km from Delhi (Approximately~06 hrs) via NH-52 and NE-4.

By Air

Jaipur Airport (JAI) is the nearest — Approximately 158 km away. Taxis available at the airport.
