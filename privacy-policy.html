Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ranthambore Ticket Booking (“We,” “Us,” or “Our”). We are an independent private safari booking assistance service and are not affiliated with the Rajasthan Forest Department or any government authority. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you access our website, send a safari enquiry, book our services, or communicate with us. By using our website and services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services to you.

A. Personal Data

While submitting an enquiry, booking a safari, requesting a quote, or contacting us, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information (“Personal Data”) that can be used to contact or identify you. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Full Name (for you and your tour members)
  • Email Address (where provided)
  • Phone Number (including WhatsApp number)
  • Safari Date, Shift and Safari Type (Gypsy or Canter)
  • Required identification details, for an actual booking

B. Booking & Transactional Data

Details about the services you request or book (such as Gypsy or Canter safari and zone preferences) and information relating to payments or transactions, where applicable.

Payments may be made through UPI using the payment details provided by us. We do not collect or store card details through our website. Payment-related information, such as transaction references or payment confirmation details, may be processed or retained as necessary to verify and manage bookings.

C. Technical and Usage Data

When you visit our website, certain technical information is collected automatically through Google Analytics and Google Ads tags (see Section 8). This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on pages, the website or advertisement you came from, and general location (such as city or region).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Manage Our Services: To process your safari enquiries and bookings, and to provide booking assistance, including communicating relevant booking information to applicable service providers or authorities where necessary.
  • For Communication: To contact you by phone or WhatsApp with updates, confirmations, safari timings, and answers to your queries.
  • Legality & Compliance: To fulfill governmental requirements for wildlife tourism within Ranthambore National Park.
  • To Understand and Improve Our Website and Advertising: To analyse how visitors use the website, measure how well our advertisements perform, and improve our content and services.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted third-party partners and authorities to ensure the delivery of our services:

  • Government Authorities (Forest Department): Where required for a safari booking, relevant booking or identification information may be shared with the applicable authorities or authorised service providers in accordance with their requirements.
  • Service Providers: We may share necessary information with safari operators, accommodation providers, payment providers or other service providers involved in fulfilling your booking request.
  • EmailJS (Enquiry Form): When you submit an enquiry form on our website, the information you provide is transmitted through EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, so that we can receive and respond to your enquiry. EmailJS may process the submitted information as necessary to provide this service.
  • Google (Analytics and Ads): Google Analytics and Google Ads receive the technical and usage data described in Section 8.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your data to comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, and fulfill government agency requests.

5. Data Security

The security of your data is very important to us. We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption and secure server access, to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or theft.

Please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep your information only as long as needed for your enquiry, booking records, support and legal requirements.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of incorrect data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data (subject to legal or booking retention requirements).

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 9.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to or use third-party services, including WhatsApp and Google Maps, that are operated independently of Ranthambore Ticket Booking.

WhatsApp may be used to allow customers to contact us regarding safari booking enquiries and related services. Google Maps may be used to display our business location or help visitors find directions.

When you access or use these third-party services, your activity and information may be handled according to the respective providers' terms, privacy policies, and data-handling practices. We do not control the privacy practices or content of external websites or services.

We encourage you to review the relevant third-party privacy policies before using these services.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, understand website performance and measure the performance of our advertising.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, device and browser details, approximate location and the source of your visit. This information is used to produce reports and improve the website.

Google Ads

We use Google Ads and the Google tag to show our advertisements on Google and to measure how our ads perform, for example by recording when a visit that started from an ad leads to an enquiry. Google may use cookies and similar identifiers for this purpose. We do not use the enquiry details you type into our form (such as your name or phone number) for advertising.

If we introduce remarketing or other advertising features in the future, we will update this policy first.

How to Control Cookies and Ads

  • You can disable or manage cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect some functionalities of our website.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • You can manage or turn off personalised advertising in your Google Ad Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
