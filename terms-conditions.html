3. Booking Process

  • Submitting an enquiry or booking request through our website, WhatsApp, email or telephone is not a confirmed booking and does not guarantee confirmation.
  • Booking requests are subject to safari availability, permit availability, applicable booking conditions and successful completion of the required booking process. A booking is confirmed only after the required payment has been successfully received and the booking has been confirmed to you.

4. Customer Responsibility

Customers must provide complete and accurate information while making a booking.

  • Full Name
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Nationality
  • Valid Government ID Details
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address (where provided)
  • Customers must report 30 minutes before safari departure

5. Identity Verification

Visitors must carry the same valid Government-issued ID that was used during booking while entering Ranthambore National Park.

Entry is subject to applicable identification and park entry requirements.

6. Pricing

Prices depend on safari type, date, zone and availability. Prices shown on the website are starting prices, and the final price is communicated to the customer before payment.

The total amount communicated and agreed upon before payment is the final amount payable for the booking. No additional charges will be collected beyond this agreed amount for the same booking. All bookings are subject to permit availability and confirmation.

7. Payment

  • Payment is required after safari availability and booking details have been confirmed. Booking confirmation is subject to successful payment and applicable permit availability.
  • Payments are accepted through UPI or other payment methods communicated by our booking team. Please verify the amount and details before you pay.
  • A booking request will not be processed until payment has been successfully received.

8. Safari Confirmation

Safari confirmation depends on permit availability, booking-system availability and applicable park rules.

We cannot guarantee confirmation for every requested booking.

9. Changes Requested by Customers

After a booking request has been submitted, modifications may be limited.

  • Date changes may not be possible.
  • Zone changes depend entirely on availability.
  • Name corrections may be restricted according to Forest Department policies.

10. Cancellation by Customer

Cancellations are governed by our Refund/Cancellation Policy and any applicable rules of the relevant authority. In summary, for a confirmed booking that you cancel:

  • Cancellation 30 days or more before the visit date: 75% refund of the total amount paid for the booking.
  • Cancellation more than 72 hours but less than 30 days before the visit date: 50% refund of the total amount paid for the booking.
  • Cancellation 72 hours or less before the visit date, or no-show: No refund.

11. Refunds

Refunds are processed according to our Refund/Cancellation Policy.

If payment has been received but we are unable to confirm or complete your safari booking from our side (for example, because of permit or safari availability issues), the full amount received for that unconfirmed booking will be refunded, with no deduction.
